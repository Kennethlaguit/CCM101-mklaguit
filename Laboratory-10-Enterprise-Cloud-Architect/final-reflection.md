# Final Reflection

## My Journey Through CCM101

Mission 10 represents the culmination of the skills I developed throughout the Cloud Computing laboratory activities.

Throughout the course, I learned about Linux, networking, Git, Docker, containers, persistent storage, security, and automation.

For the final mission, I applied these concepts to design and deploy my own multi-tier infrastructure.

## What I Learned

One of the most important lessons I learned was that cloud infrastructure requires more than simply running an application. It also requires security, persistence, automation, monitoring, documentation, and recovery planning.

## Challenges

During the development of this project, I encountered technical challenges involving configuration, networking, Docker containers, storage, and security.

I resolved these challenges by analyzing errors, reviewing documentation, testing commands, and applying what I learned from previous laboratory activities.

## Mission 10 Experience

This project helped me understand the responsibilities of a cloud infrastructure architect and how different technologies work together to create a functional infrastructure.

## Conclusion

Mission 10 allowed me to combine the knowledge and practical skills developed throughout CCM101 into one complete project.
